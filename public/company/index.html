why we exist

Ten days. Then everybody flies home.

You swap numbers. You make the group chat. Three weeks later it is dead — and the best friends you made that year are people you now watch on a feed.

Everyone has lost a group like that. That is the thing this company exists to stop.

what we are

One company, two halves

Neither one is a side project. Each is how the other gets its people.

Travellers Outdoors

Eco-friendly trips, beach and desert clean-ups, and psychodrama sessions that turn a bus full of strangers into a group. The trip is free to join — we earn on the activities and the accommodation.

لمّة · the live quiz

Five languages. One question. The same second.

The host presents on a screen shaped for a television. Everybody else answers on their own phone — an Egyptian reading Arabic and a Romanian reading Romanian, on the same question, at the same moment.

📺 Presenter mode

A 16:9 picture made for a TV, a projector or a screen-share.

📱 Everyone plays

The host reads. The room answers, all at once, on their own phones.

🌍 Five play languages

Arabic, English, French, Spanish and Romanian — together, not one at a time.

🔒 Nobody can cheat

Scores are worked out by the server, never by the phone. The winner really won.
